    Payoff order

      Timeline

      Smallest balance first. Interest is added monthly (balance × APR ÷ 12) before each payment. When a debt hits $0, its minimum rolls into the next one. Month 1 is next month. Real statements differ a little: daily interest, fees, and rate changes aren’t modeled.

      Why smallest first, when the math says highest rate first?

      Paying the highest APR first (the “avalanche”) usually costs less interest. The snowball trades a little of that for something most plans run out of by month three: proof it’s working. Clearing a whole balance early frees up a minimum payment and gives you a win you can see. If you’re the kind of person who stops opening statements when it gets hard, the plan you’ll actually stick with beats the cheaper one you’ll abandon.
